隱私權政策

最後更新:2026 年 10 月 15 日

English

簡單說:tterminal 不蒐集你的任何資料。

存在你裝置上的資料

你新增的主機(名稱、位址、帳號、密碼)、SSH 金鑰和 app 設定,都只存在你的裝置上,開發者拿不到。

SSH 連線

連線是你的裝置直接連到你指定的主機,中間不經過開發者的任何伺服器。

同步

同步預設是關閉的。打開的話,資料只會送到你自己設定的伺服器,而且在離開裝置前就已經用 XChaCha20-Poly1305 加密,伺服器只看得到密文。開發者沒有營運任何同步伺服器。

沒有的東西

沒有帳號、沒有分析或追蹤工具、沒有廣告、沒有第三方 SDK、沒有當機回報。

Live Activity

app 在背景時,動態島與鎖定畫面顯示的連線數量只在你的裝置上計算與顯示,不會送到任何地方。

聯絡

對隱私權有疑問,請到 GitHub Issues 留言。

Privacy Policy (English)

In short: tterminal doesn't collect any of your data.

Data on your device

The hosts you add (names, addresses, usernames, passwords), your SSH keys and your settings are stored only on your device. The developer has no access to them.

SSH connections

Your device connects directly to the hosts you choose. Nothing passes through any server run by the developer.

Sync

Sync is off by default. If you turn it on, data goes only to a server you configure yourself, and it's encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305 before it leaves the device, so that server only sees ciphertext. The developer doesn't operate any sync servers.

What isn't there

No accounts, no analytics or tracking, no ads, no third-party SDKs, no crash reporting.

Live Activity

The connection count shown in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen is computed and displayed on your device only.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
